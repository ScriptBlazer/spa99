{% comment %}The shared calendar, range mode: one field that picks a first and a last day (docs/features/calendar.md). Put the assets (_date_picker_assets.html) in `head_extra`. Context: id (unique), label, label_class (optional), empty (the field's words while nothing is chosen), from and to (dates or empty, the dates now chosen), min and max (ISO text or empty: the first and last day that can be picked), format (the System date format for flatpickr, `date_format` in the Office pages' context), note (optional: a line under the calendar), sheet (true when the page moves this field into a phone sheet: the calendar then lies open in place). Without JavaScript the two real date inputs below ARE the control and the Apply button sends them; with it they hide, stay in the form, and the one field takes over. They are named `from` and `to` and always hold ISO dates. A single date in an ordinary Django form is not this include: give its input `data-date-picker data-mode="single" data-format="..."` (see system.forms.RevenueForm).{% endcomment %}
