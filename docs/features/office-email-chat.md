# Email chat in the Office (plan, 2026-11-04)

Status: **built and reviewed** (2026-11-04, branch `feature/whatsapp`, not released): server side, the Office UI ("Built, UI"), the review fixes, and "Office email from a person" (last section: personal sender, signature, copy to the group, Outlook folding). Postmark inbound and Delivery are only tested with payloads written from Postmark's documentation. Decisions in PROJECT.md (2026-11-04 rows).

## Goal
Staff have two clearly separate ways to talk to a person: **WhatsApp** and **Email**. Email is a real chat: staff write in the Office, he replies from his normal mailbox, and the reply lands in the Office thread.

This reverses 2026-11-03 "email Message goes away, staff messages go by WhatsApp only". Automatic emails and Send guide are unchanged.

## How a reply gets back (Postmark inbound)
- Each thread has a random token. Office emails go from hello@ with `Reply-To: reply+<token>@<EMAIL_INBOUND_DOMAIN>`.
- Postmark receives mail for that domain and POSTs JSON to our inbound webhook. `MailboxHash` holds the token; `StrippedTextReply` holds only his new text (fall back to `TextBody`; if there is only `HtmlBody`, strip it to text). `MessageID` makes the webhook idempotent. Attachments arrive base64.
- Postmark retries on anything but 200, and stops on 403 (10 retries, up to 6 hours apart).
- `EMAIL_INBOUND_DOMAIN` blank: Reply-To stays hello@, the Office still sends and shows sent emails, and the screen says replies go to the hello@ mailbox.

## Screens
- **Reach out card** (lead, person, Gold): two labelled parts, WhatsApp and Email, each with its own button and status. A channel that cannot be used stays visible and says why.
- **Email** in the Office navigation: inbox and thread, in the same family as WhatsApp but visibly a different channel.
- Thread: his and our emails, subject on the first and when it changes, attachments, delivery state, bounce shown plainly. Staff can write at any time (no 24-hour window).
- The assistant chat tab is renamed **Assistant** so "chats" no longer means three things.

## Rules
- Viewing: `reader`. Sending: `editor` (Approver or Admin and up), same as WhatsApp.
- Text only is rendered: no HTML from an email ever reaches the page. Attachments download, they never open inline, except images and PDFs.
- A Gold email sent from the application marks New as Contacted, once, like WhatsApp.
- Replies notify staff by the same push and mute setting as WhatsApp.
- Reply from another address (forwarded mail): stored, and the bubble shows that address.
- Spam flagged by Postmark (`X-Spam-Status: Yes`): stored and marked, no push.
- UK GDPR: threads, messages and files are in staff delete and export. Member self-service includes a thread only when his account's email is verified and equals the thread address.

## Mini Outlook (Moishe, 2026-11-04, replaces the plain text parts above)
- **Buttons on every lead, person and Gold page, where Message used to be:** one for WhatsApp and one for email, always both. The wording shows the state: "Start WhatsApp chat" or "Open WhatsApp chat", "Start email" or "Open email thread".
- **Email tab in the side navigation, like WhatsApp:** thread list on the left, the open email large on the right. Phone: list, then the email.
- **Several threads per person,** each with its own subject and reply token, like Outlook. The page button opens his latest thread, or a new email if he has none.
- **New email** from the Email tab: search for a known person or type an address, then subject and body. Reply from inside a thread.
- **Rich text:** bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight, text colour, bulleted and numbered lists, links, quotes, attachments. The server cleans the HTML to an allow-list and always sends a plain text copy too.
- **His emails display as formatted,** with HTML cleaned on the server, shown in a sandboxed frame with no scripts, and outside images blocked until staff click Show images. Earlier quoted text collapses.
- List filters: All, Unread, Sent with no reply. Search by name, address and subject.
- Not in this round: Bcc, Forward, shared folders, server-side drafts (an unsent draft stays in that browser).

# Built (server side, 2026-11-04)

App `emailchat` (models, `services.py` for all logic, `webhooks.py`, `views.py`, `tasks.py`, `context.py`, `admin.py`). Shared helper `common/richtext.py` (cleaning and plain text). `nh3==0.3.7` added. Deviation: no `django-simple-history` on these models (messages are the log; copies would double what must be erased). Minimal placeholder templates in `emailchat/templates/emailchat/` (`inbox.html`, `_rows.html`, `thread.html`, `new.html`) for the frontend to replace.

## Settings (server `.env`, all optional)
`EMAIL_INBOUND_DOMAIN` (blank = replies go to hello@, nothing lands in the Office), `EMAIL_INBOUND_USER`, `EMAIL_INBOUND_PASSWORD` (the inbound webhook is 404 until both are set and the domain is set). Limits in settings: file 10 MB, 9 MB of files per email we send, 20 files, whole inbound POST 50 MB. Startup line: `emailchat=replies-in-office | replies-to-mailbox | inbound-webhook-not-set`.

## Models
`Thread` (UUID; `email` lower case, NOT unique: many threads per address; `token` unique random 24 hex; `subject` the first one; owner links `user` / `lead_email` / `application` kept for erasure; `name`; `last_message_at`, `last_inbound_at`, `last_preview`, `last_direction`, `has_attachment`), `ThreadRead` (per staff member), `Message` (integer id; direction in/out; `subject`, `shows_subject`, `body_text`, `body_full`, `body_html` cleaned, `has_remote_images`; author; status queued, sent, delivered, bounced, failed, received; `provider_id` unique (Postmark MessageID); `rfc_message_id`, `in_reply_to`, `references`; `from_address`, `from_name`; `spam`; `note`; error code and message; `claimed_at`; `legacy_person_message`), `Attachment` (storage key, filename, served type, declared type, size, `content_id`, `inline_ok`, `checked`). Files are in the default storage (S3 on live) under `emailchat/<thread id>/<random>`.

## Sending
`services.send` cleans the HTML (`common.richtext.clean_outgoing`: p, br, strong, b, em, i, u, s, mark, span with only `color` and `background-color` values that are plain colours, ul, ol, li, a with http, https and mailto only and `rel`/`target` set, blockquote, h2, h3), makes the plain text copy, stores files, queues `emailchat.tasks.send_message` (id only). The worker (`services.deliver`, claimed once, never twice) calls `notifications.services.send_templated("office_message", ..., html_body=..., plain_body=...)` from the From stored on the message at queue time (the staff member's own address, see "Office email from a person"; before that it was hello@). `send_templated` gained `html_body`: cleaned AGAIN there with the same allow-list before it is marked safe, and a `html_body` key in the `context` argument is ignored, so no existing path changed. The suppression guard still runs: a suppressed address comes back as **failed**, `error.reason` "Not sent. This address bounced before or marked an email as spam, so nothing more can be sent to it.", no retry. A duplicate (same body, same person, within 10 seconds) is refused. A subject left blank becomes "Re: <last subject>" (the thread's own subject while it is empty). Threading headers: our own `Message-ID`, `In-Reply-To` and `References` from his last mail that carried a Message-ID, `X-PM-Tag: office-chat` and `X-PM-Metadata-chat: <message id>` (SMTP gives no Postmark MessageID at send time, so the metadata is how a bounce finds the message). Never sent: files named .exe, .bat, .js and the like (`bad_file`).
- Reply-To `reply+<token>@<EMAIL_INBOUND_DOMAIN>`; with the domain blank Reply-To is the contact (hello@) address and context carries `replies_to_mailbox: true` (the screen says replies go to the mailbox).
- Gold: sending from a Gold thread (`gold=<application id>` posted) turns New into Contacted once, with a `responded` event, `meta {"email": true}` (`gold.actions.whatsapp_sent(..., via="email")`); the Gold history says "Marked Contacted: an email went to the applicant."

## Inbound (Postmark)
`POST /postmark/inbound/` on the PUBLIC host (`config/urls.py`, next to `postmark/webhook/`). HTTP Basic with `EMAIL_INBOUND_USER` and `_PASSWORD`; 404 until configured; 401 wrong or missing credentials. Reads the body with `request.read()` (not `.body`: Django's 2.5 MB check would refuse a mail with files). Over 50 MB: 200 and a log line. Not JSON: 400. **Always 200 for anything we choose not to store** (unknown, missing or malformed `MailboxHash`, no `MessageID`), logged without personal data. Idempotent on `MessageID`. Text = `StrippedTextReply`, else `TextBody`, else the HTML stripped (`body_full` keeps the whole `TextBody` when it differs). `HtmlBody` is cleaned by `richtext.clean_incoming` and stored. Sender: `FromFull.Email` (else `From`) stored as `from_address`; the message dict has `from_other` when it is not the thread address. Spam (`X-Spam-Status` starting Yes): stored and flagged, never pushed, never counted unread, never moves the thread.
- **How attachment bytes reach the worker:** the web process decodes each file and writes it straight to the default storage (S3 on live; web and worker share no disk, so storage is the one safe place) and the task queue only ever gets the message id. If storage fails the webhook removes the half-stored email and answers 500, so Postmark's retry stores it whole. The worker (`emailchat.tasks.process_inbound`) then checks each file's first bytes and sends the push. Over 10 MB, undecodable or past 20 files: dropped, with a note on the message (`note`). Only an image (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP) or PDF whose first bytes match its declared type is `inline_ok`; everything else downloads as `application/octet-stream`.
- Push: `whatsapp.push.notify_payload` (the same devices and the same mute setting) with `{title: "Email from <name>", body: "<subject>: <first line>", url: "/email/<thread>/", tag: "email-<thread>"}`.
- **Bounces:** `notifications.postmark.apply` calls `emailchat.services.apply_feedback` for Bounce, SpamComplaint and Delivery records before its own logic (which is unchanged, including the suppression list and the bounce log that drives "Possible fake"). The chat message named by `Metadata.chat`, and only when the recipient is the thread's address, becomes `bounced` (reason "Bounced. The address did not accept this email.") or, on a spam complaint, "Marked as spam by the recipient". Transient, AutoResponder and DnsError bounces change nothing. A Delivery record moves `sent` to `delivered` (tick Delivery in Postmark). Never raises.

## His HTML (the frame)
`GET /email/message/<message id>/frame/` (`reader`, his messages with HTML only): a complete document for `<iframe sandbox="allow-same-origin allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox" src=...>` (no `allow-scripts`; `allow-same-origin` only so the page can read the height to size the frame, which is safe with no scripts). Never put his HTML in the Office page. `?images=1` loads outside pictures (staff chose Show images); without it they are removed and the header `X-Blocked-Images: <n>` says how many. `cid:` pictures come from the stored files as `data:` addresses (up to 1 MB each, 5 MB in all). Headers: CSP `default-src 'none'; img-src data: [https: http: with images=1]; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; font-src 'none'; form-action 'none'; base-uri 'none'; frame-ancestors 'self'; sandbox allow-same-origin allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox` (no script source at all), `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, `nosniff`, no cache. Links carry `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"`. **Quoted history:** the cleaner keeps `<blockquote>` and turns Gmail and Yahoo quote containers into `class="quoted"`; collapsing is left to the frontend (the frame is same origin, so the page script can find `blockquote, .quoted` and fold them). Outlook's quoted blocks have no marker we can rely on, so those are not marked.

# Review fixes (2026-11-04)

- **A corrected lead address moves his threads.** `outreach.services.correct_lead_email` calls `emailchat.services.move_address(old, new)` in its transaction: `Thread.email` and `lead_email` change, the reply token does not (a late reply to the old Reply-To still lands in the same thread). A thread owned by an account is left alone. So the lead page says Open, the export and the erasure find the thread. A bounce for an email sent to the old address no longer matches the thread (it checks the thread's address) and is ignored by the chat; the suppression list still gets it.
- **Only a verified account is linked** (`resolve_links`): an unverified account (anyone can register any address) never takes a thread's owner link or name, and staff export or erase of an unverified account uses the owner link only, never the address.
- **Inbound is finished or retried, never half done.** `record_inbound` stores the message with `incomplete=True` (and moves the thread in the same transaction). The webhook stores the files, queues `process_inbound`, and only then clears `incomplete`. A failure answers 500; Postmark's retry of the same MessageID resumes: it takes the message with one conditional update on `claimed_at` (an attempt less than two minutes old keeps it), clears any files stored before, stores them again from the payload (so nothing is doubled) and queues again. `process_inbound` takes the push with `notified_at`, so the team is pushed once. Possible in the rare case: a worker that dies after it took the push but before it sent loses that one push; it is never sent twice.
- **A send claimed by a worker that died** (status queued, `claimed_at` older than 10 minutes, longer than the worker's job limit and all retries) shows "This may not have been sent" with Try again. The never-sent-twice guard is kept (the conditional update on `claimed_at`, so a double click retries once), but it cannot hold in one rare case: the worker died after the mail server accepted the email and before SENT was written. Then Try again sends it a second time, which is why the wording says to check with him first. WhatsApp has the same gap and the same treatment (`whatsapp.services.is_unsure`).
- **New email:** a failed first send removes the empty thread; `latest_thread` ignores threads with no message (so "Open email thread" is never shown for nothing).
- **Message-ID, In-Reply-To and References are stored and read without whitespace** (`clean_id`), so a header with a newline cannot make a reply fail with BadHeaderError. The reply subject is trimmed to 300 after "Re: ". A blank From counts as a different sender. The text-only fallback in the page is escaped (`linebreaks(autoescape=True)`).
- **Export** now holds `full_text` (his whole text with the quoted mail), `from_name` and a listing of files (name, type, size; not the bytes). Django admin delete of a Message or Attachment removes the files from storage.
- **Index:** `(thread, direction, created_at)` on the message (`emailchat_unread_idx`, migration `0002`): the unread counts, the inbox filter and the badge all filter on it. A new migration, not an edit of `0001`, because `0001` is already the schema others may have run. `0002` also adds `incomplete` and `notified_at` (database defaults, no backfill).
- Plain text copy of an email: numbered lists keep their numbers, `mailto:` addresses are kept, every line of a quote starts "> ".
- People search is rate limited (120 a minute per address).

# Review fixes, round 2 (2026-11-04)

- **Hostile HTML mail:** the plain text copy (`common.richtext.html_to_text`) is one pass (lines are collected with their quote depth, no scanning back or forward over them) and a quote shows at most 8 `> `. `clean_incoming` refuses markup that is `too_complex`: longer than `MAX_HTML` (300,000 characters), or more than 50,000 `<` characters, or nested deeper than 400 (one linear pass that jumps from `<` to `>`). The message then keeps its text (from the raw HTML) and a note says the layout was too complicated. Why these three: nh3's time is quadratic in some shapes whatever the depth (`<div/>`, `<div <>`, mis-nested `<span><div></span></div>`), which the tag budget bounds; and it is quadratic in the number of different attributes on one tag (203,000 of them in 1 MB took 33 s), which no count of `<` or `>` can bound because a quoted `>` hides the tags, so the size cap does. Measured through the webhook (the timing test, 21 shapes at 1 MB and just under the cap, attribute floods included): the worst is about 2.8 s (attributes just under the cap, at the 300,000 character cap), every 1 MB input is refused in under 0.3 s. Margin for real mail: a newsletter is 20 to 100 KB; one with 18,000 `<` is about 100 KB and passes, so the size cap leaves about 3 times that. Mail over 300 KB loses its layout, not its text. Cleaning stays in the webhook: moving it to the worker would need a "cleaning pending" state and a retry rule for a message already shown, which is more machinery than a size cap. Staff-composed mail (`clean_outgoing`) is not depth-checked. A line holding only a typed `>` is kept.
- **Rate limiter** (`common.ratelimit.hit`, shared by every caller): see "Rate limit modes" below (round 3). Django's `incr` cannot take a timeout and the database cache resets it to 300 s, so no mode uses it. The New email people search shows "Too many searches just now" on a 429.
- **Unverified account:** `common.ownership.trusted_addresses` passes no addresses for it (this replaced `emailchat.addresses_for`), so its page, its start button, its timeline and its staff export and erasure never touch a lead's thread; the timeline's email log also leaves out staff-written mail for it.
- **`move_address`** (final rule in round 3): a thread linked to a Gold application does not move on a lead's correction (unless the application is already on the new address), and `lead_email` on account-owned threads is left alone.
- **Delivery** also corrects a queued message (a worker that died before writing SENT): no Try again after proof of delivery. # Review fixes, round 3 (2026-11-04)

- **Rate limit modes** (`hit(scope, who, limit, window, rolling=False, lock=False)`). *Fixed*: the key carries the window number; cheap; a burst of up to twice the limit can pass at an edge. *Rolling*: one cache key holds the times of the last `limit` allowed hits and expires a window after the last write; never more than `limit` in any rolling window, honest traffic spread out is never refused (the old "sliding" count moved the expiry on every hit, so one hit every 30 minutes was refused from hit 21). Refused hits are not recorded. *Rolling with lock* (guessing): the first refusal locks the caller, and every attempt after that, refused ones included, is refused until a whole window passes with no attempts. Get then set is not atomic: worst case, a burst of N parallel requests that all read before any writes lets up to N-1 extra through once. Per caller: login 10/5 min and password change 10/h and system code: rolling + lock; register 20/h, forgot password 5/h, resend verification 5/h, Gold apply 10 valid/h, Gold invite setup POST 20/h, account emails, the System email and list screens: rolling; people search, chat, journey, Postmark webhook, unsubscribe GET and bad tokens (30/min), invite link (60/min), Gold invite setup GET (60/min, its own key): fixed. **A valid signed unsubscribe POST is not rate limited at all** (a legal duty; it is idempotent and needs a token only we can sign), so a mail scanner on the same address can never block it.
- **Unverified account and WhatsApp:** `user.mobile` is unproven. Superseded by the Ownership rule below: `person_phones` is gone, no lookup uses a number, and `resolve_links` links by number only where the rule allows.
- **Gold-linked thread on a lead correction:** `correct_lead_email` does not change the application, so moving its thread would send later staff replies to an address that is not the applicant's, without a word. The rule: such a thread stays on the applicant's address, where the applicant's export and erasure find it; a thread with no application moves with the lead (his export and erasure by the new address find it). `threads_for_person` also finds threads through the person's Gold applications (the account's, or made with his addresses), so a thread already moved on another address is still exported and erased.
- **Merge** (`users.merge._move_chats`) moves email threads and WhatsApp chats before the removed account is deleted: the owner link goes to keep (the delete would have nulled it), the removed address on the chat becomes keep's, chats linked to a moved application follow it, and chats that only share a verified removed address move too. A chat owned by another account is left alone.

# Ownership: one rule for chats and threads (2026-11-04, review round 5)

Reviews kept finding the same bug: who owns a chat or thread was decided in many places, some trusting an unproven detail (an unverified account's address, a typed mobile number, an `application.user` link made by address alone). The rule now lives in one module, `common/ownership.py`, used by both `emailchat` and `whatsapp`. Every lookup for a page, a timeline, an export, an erasure and a merge goes through it (`emailchat.services.threads_for`, `whatsapp.services.contacts_for_person`, `users.merge._chats_of`, `office.profile`).

A person's threads and chats are exactly:
1. Those with an **explicit owner link** (`user`), set by a staff action (an email started from his person page, the thread's "Link it?" button, a merge) or by a proven step (the verified address).
2. Those **on his address** (`Thread.email`, `lead_email`, `Contact.lead_email`), only when his account email is verified. A person with no account (a lead page, a Gold applicant) is found by the address staff opened.
3. Those **through his Gold application**, only when that application's link is proven: the account is verified, or staff is on the application's own page.

**The link rule (round 6; it sits in the docstring of `common/ownership.py`).** A chat's links (`user`, `lead_email`, `application`) are set only by: a) a staff POST by an editor after seeing the chat (the thread's "This number isn't linked to X. Link it?" button, `POST /whatsapp/<id>/link/`), b) creating a NEW chat from a staff start action (a POST), or c) a proven email address joining up on verify, only when the chat already has that lead address (set by a or b). A number typed on a public form (journey gate, Register, Gold apply) never sets or replaces a link, or a name, on an existing chat; it stays on its own form record. **No GET ever writes a link or creates ownership**: Open is a link, Start is a form POST with CSRF, the email start views only read (`/…/email-chat/` opens the latest thread or the New email screen; the thread is created by the POST on that screen). Before this, the public journey gate overwrote a chat's `lead_email` by number, Gold apply set `application` on a verified member's chat, and a read-only GET on "Open WhatsApp chat" claimed the chat, so staff export and delete then reached someone else's messages.

Also:
- **A mobile number proves nothing.** No lookup uses a number. `whatsapp.resolve_links` is now only rule 1c: a chat that already has a staff-set lead address joins the account whose verified email is that address, and takes its name from what it is linked to. `whatsapp.relink_address` runs it on verify. The old `relink(phones)` is gone: no public form calls into chats.
- **Verifying an email proves the email only.** On verify, `link_user_applications` links the applications made with that address, `relink_address` and `emailchat.relink` refresh the chats and threads that already carry it, and a ticked news box lifts an earlier unsubscribe (not at Register: an unverified account may be a stranger's).
- **A start from a page** creates the chat linked to that page's person when there is none (`whatsapp.services.start_chat`); an existing chat is opened untouched and the thread offers the link (`ownership.link`; a person's page goes through `claim`). A chat that has an owner is never taken, and a lead's chat is not claimed unless the address is the verified account's own. A correction of a lead's address moves the chats that hold the old address (`whatsapp.services.move_address`).
- **An unlinked chat on a person's mobile** is shown on his page (`visible_from_page`) but is not his for export or delete. The page's Data card, the delete confirmation and the delete's success message name it, the staff export lists it by number and name (`whatsapp_chats_not_included`, never the messages), and an Admin deletes it from the chat (`Delete this chat`, POST, audited without text).
- **A page shows a chat as the person's** only when the rule holds for it, or the chat belongs to nobody yet (`visible_from_page`).
- **Gold: `gold.services.submit_application` links `application.user` only to a verified account** (the old code linked any account with that address, so a stranger who registered with the victim's address, unverified, got the victim's application). The link is made when the account verifies (`link_earlier_data`). Nothing on Gold needed the unverified link: the invite and approval flows (`gold/invites.py`) look the account up by address themselves, `resend_invite` already refuses when any account has the address, and `gold_since` and the Gold call to action only matter for a real member. `application.user` rows already on live are not rewritten; lookups never trust them for an unverified account.
- **Merge** (`users/merge.py`): applications move only when the removed account is verified (linked to it, or made with its address); the same for the chats that hang on them. A removed account that is unverified moves only the chats that carry its owner link, and the address on a chat or thread is rewritten to keep's only for a verified removed account, because a thread's replies go to its address.
- **Gold Delete** erases the email threads linked to that application and the WhatsApp chats staff linked to it, unless an account owns them (his page, export and erasure reach those). A WhatsApp chat is erased only when it has the applicant's own address as its lead address (the Gold "Link it?" gives it that), and never when a verified account holds the address, the application or the chat: a chat tied only through a typed number goes nowhere. An applicant with no account has no person or lead page, so before this his stays-behind thread was reached by no Office erasure. There is no Gold-page export; if one is added it must call `threads_for(application=...)` and `contacts_for_person(application=...)`.
- **Self-service stays stricter:** a member's own download and delete use only the thread(s) on his verified account address (`threads_for_member`) and never a WhatsApp chat.
