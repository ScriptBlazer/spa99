# Spa 99 — Pricing

Source: `Spa 99 - Membership and Pricing.pdf` (14 Oct 2026) and the rules spec (16 Oct 2026).

**These are today's values, not code.** In the system every number here is a field on a `Plan` (or a setting) that Moishe edits in Spa 99 Control. Prices change; history is kept.

All prices exclude VAT unless stated. Spa 99 is VAT registered. VAT rate (20%) is a setting.

## Per-visit illustrations (explanation only, never charged)

- Tier 1: about £50 a visit at 6 visits.
- Tier 2: about £50 a visit at 8 visits. The extra £100 over Tier 1 buys evening access, two more visits and a longer window.
- Tier 3: about £42 a visit at 12 visits.

## Extras

- **Leil Shishi** (option, not confirmed): Thursday evening chef's meal, £45 + VAT a place, Tier 2/3, included for Gold. Food cost unknown.
- **Merch**: website and on site, not vending. Water bottle, tote bag etc. Gold gets each new release free.
- **Vending**: food, snacks, drinks whenever the building is open.
