What it means
The day we assume members start paying, while the spa has no opening date.
What it changes
When each member's 12 monthly payments fall due, so which dates hold money on the Revenue calendar. It applies only to members with no start date of their own. The Gold Card fee falls due on this day too.
What it never changes
The real yearly figure, which is the same whatever the date. Anything on the website.
Example
Set it to 1 February 2027. A member on £400 a month with no start date of his own owes 12 payments, from 1 February 2027 to 1 January 2028. Choose March 2027 on the Revenue page and he adds £400.
Public or private
This date is private: only the Revenue page uses it. The opening date is public: it is set in Settings and shown on the website. Once the opening date is set, it takes over and this date is ignored.
{% include "system/_field_row.html" with field=form.revenue_paying_percent unit="%" %}
What it means
Of the people counted as approved, the share you expect to really pay. 100% means everyone.
What it changes
The figure labelled With your assumptions, for memberships and the Gold Card, and the same figure for people waiting.
What it never changes
The real figure, any price, or anyone's status.
Example
A real figure of £100,000 at 80% gives £80,000 with your assumptions. The real figure still reads £100,000.
{% include "system/_field_row.html" with field=form.revenue_dropoff_percent unit="%" %}
What it means
Of those paying, the share you expect to leave each month. 0% means nobody leaves.
What it changes
The figure labelled With your assumptions, for memberships. It builds up: each member's month 1 counts in full, month 2 is reduced once, month 3 twice, and so on.
What it never changes
The real figure, any price, or the Gold Card, which is one yearly fee.
Example
£400 a month at 5%: month 1 is £400, month 2 £380, month 3 £361, month 12 about £228. Over 12 months that is about £3,677 instead of £4,800. With 80% paying as well, about £2,942.
