An estimate, never money received (docs/features/office-2-design.md section 3). Context: see office.views_approvals.revenue_page. Whole pounds are for display only (`pounds`): the figures are exact pence, and no rounded figure is ever added up. Two states. With no dates the page is the full estimate and never mentions a date (the assumed start date appears only in the calendar's note). With dates every figure answers one question, what is due in those dates: the year and month columns are replaced by one "In these dates" figure, never averaged. "With your assumptions" (`assumed_shown`) is a second figure in a different material, shown only when an assumption is set and the calendar is stored. It never changes the real figure. The Possible panel (people still waiting) is never added to the approved figures and is a different material (sunken, no shadow, no big number) so it cannot be read as part of the money. The four-figure grid (_revenue_grid.html) and the range grid (_revenue_range_grid.html) are the page's two grids. The by-plan cards are the phone's; the one table below replaces them on a computer. The full breakdown (_revenue_breakdown.html) is the last card, closed until opened: the sum read top to bottom, ending on the full 12-month figure, with the waiting people after it. Its figures never change with dates or assumptions.{% endcomment %}
